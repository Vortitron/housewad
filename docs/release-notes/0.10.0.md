## Draw your floor plan in the card

- **A plan editor in the card's settings.** Open the card's settings and press *Draw the plan*. It starts from a sketch of your Home Assistant areas, one block of rooms per floor. Drag each room to where it really is and pull a corner to size it. You can add a part for an L-shaped room, mark gardens, and trace over a photo or drawing of your real plan. The game in the preview rebuilds as you go.
- **A plan of rooms is enough.** Doors go wherever two rooms share a metre of wall, with one out to each garden drawn against the house. Stairs join each floor to the next, and upper floors find their own place in the map. Click a wall in the editor's *Doors* view to choose doors yourself. Plans that already list their doors are left exactly as they are.
- **Plans from Mosaic work as they are.** It uses the same plan format.

## On a phone

- The overhead map takes your fingers. Pinch to zoom, drag to move it, tap a lamp to switch it, double-tap a room to go there. The game's touch keys step aside while the map is open.

## Fixes

- **Lamps are never landed on.** *Follow* and map travel teleported you to a spot that was only checked for demons, never for lamps. A Doom teleport destroys whatever it lands on, so arriving on a lamp could switch that light off for real. Both now land only on a clear spot.
- **Follow no longer gets stuck.** Its walk to where your phone is used to stop at anything solid in the way. Now, if it stops getting closer, it hops to the spot once that spot is clear.
